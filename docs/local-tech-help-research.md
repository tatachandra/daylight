## Suggested validation from the earlier discussion

1. Interview five older adults and five adult children about a recent real technology problem and how they solved it.
2. Check providers’ actual coverage, availability, and prices instead of assuming a competitive gap.
3. Try a clearly scoped offer with five paying households; track setup, travel, support, follow-up time, and repeat requests.

## Service approach

Customers initiate requests or agree to appointments. Remote sessions require permission; customers enter their own passwords and verification codes. Avoid unattended access by default. Confirm travel, scope, and costs before starting. Use appropriately licensed software for paid remote support. Suspicious-message assistance is guidance, not a guarantee of safety.
